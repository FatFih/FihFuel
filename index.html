FihFuel

FihFuel is a website made to publish custom Monster Energy drink can and flavour designs. These designs are created by hand and are enhanced by AI to make it look more realistic. Eventually our plan is to sell these designs to Monster Energy and to other energy drink companies once we get the license to use their image and won't get copyright struck by them. The website currently doesn't have a custom domain name because it is a starter project and once we get our first deal from Monster Energy or any other company we will get rights for the domain so that the website can be accessed more easily around the globe.
